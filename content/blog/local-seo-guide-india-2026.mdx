## Setting Up and Optimising Google Business Profile in 10 Steps

Your Google Business Profile India listing is the foundation of local visibility. Here's how to set it up correctly:

### Steps 1-5: Foundation Setup

**Step 1: Claim or create your listing.** Go to business.google.com and search for your business. If it exists, claim it. If not, create a new profile. Use your exact legal business name — adding keywords like "Best Salon" violates guidelines and risks suspension.

**Step 2: Choose the right primary category.** This is crucial. Google offers over 4,000 categories. Pick the most specific one that matches your core offering. A tutoring centre should choose "Tutoring Service" not "Educational Institution". You can add secondary categories, but the primary one carries most weight.

**Step 3: Define your service area accurately.** For storefront businesses, enter your complete address with pincode. For service-area businesses, specify cities or pincodes you actually serve. Don't claim all of Delhi NCR if you only work in Noida and Ghaziabad.

**Step 4: Complete verification.** Phone verification is fastest for eligible categories. Keep your registered mobile active. Postcard verification takes 7-14 days in most Indian cities — don't change anything on your profile while waiting.

**Step 5: Add accurate business hours.** Include special hours for holidays and local festivals. Indian customers check hours before visiting — wrong information means lost footfall and negative reviews.

### Steps 6-10: Optimisation

**Step 6: Write a compelling business description.** Use all 750 characters. Include your location, primary services, and what makes you different. Natural keyword inclusion helps, but write for humans first.

**Step 7: Upload 20+ high-quality photos.** Include your storefront (helps customers recognise you), interior, team, products, and work samples. Businesses with photos receive 42% more direction requests. Geo-tag images before uploading.

**Step 8: Add all services with descriptions.** List each service you offer with pricing where possible. Indian customers compare options — detailed service information reduces friction.

**Step 9: Enable messaging and set up Q&A.** Respond to messages within 24 hours or Google may disable the feature. Pre-populate Q&A with common questions and detailed answers.

**Step 10: Post weekly updates.** Google Business Profile posts expire after 7 days. Share offers, events, tips, or behind-the-scenes content. This signals activity and gives you more SERP real estate.

<Callout type="tip">

## NAP Consistency Across Indian Directories

NAP stands for Name, Address, Phone — and inconsistency across the web confuses Google about your business identity. If JustDial shows "Sharma Electronics" at "Shop 12, Main Market" while Sulekha shows "Sharma Electronic's" at "Shop No. 12, Main Mkt", Google isn't sure if these are the same business or two different shops.

### Priority Indian Directories for Citations

Start with the platforms that matter most for local search ranking India:

**JustDial** — Still the largest Indian local directory with strong domain authority. Claim your free listing, complete every field, and upload photos. Paid listings offer prominence but aren't necessary for citation value.

**Sulekha** — Particularly strong for service businesses. Their lead generation model means active businesses appear higher, so respond to any enquiries.

**IndiaMART** — Essential for B2B and manufacturing businesses. Even if you don't actively sell here, a complete listing helps citation consistency.

**Google Maps** — Yes, this is part of your GBP, but ensure embedded maps on your website match exactly.

**Industry-specific directories** — Practo for healthcare, Zomato for restaurants, UrbanClap (Urban Company) for home services. These carry significant weight in their categories.

### Maintaining Consistency

Create a master document with your exact business name, complete address (including floor, building name, landmark), phone numbers, and website URL. Use this exact format everywhere. When listing on a new directory, copy-paste from this document.

Audit existing citations quarterly. Search your phone number in quotes on Google to find old listings. Update or remove outdated information. Tools like BrightLocal or Moz Local can help, though manual checking remains more accurate for Indian directories.

## Review Generation Without Violating Google Policy

Reviews directly impact local search ranking India and conversion rates. A business with 4.5 stars and 80 reviews will outrank a 5-star business with 3 reviews — and convert visitors better too.

### What Google Prohibits

Before discussing strategy, understand the rules. Google explicitly bans: paying for reviews (directly or through discounts), review gating (only asking happy customers), fake reviews from employees or agencies, and bulk solicitation that feels automated. Violations result in review removal or listing suspension.

### Ethical Review Generation Strategies

**Ask at the moment of satisfaction.** Train your team to request reviews when customers express happiness — right after a successful delivery, at checkout with a smile, or following a compliment. Timing matters more than volume.

**Make it effortless.** Create a short URL (g.page/yourbusiness/review) and display it on receipts, WhatsApp signatures, and follow-up messages. Every extra click loses 50% of potential reviewers.

**Respond to every review.** Thank positive reviewers specifically (mention what they appreciated). Address negative reviews professionally — your response is for future customers reading, not just the complainant. Businesses that respond to reviews receive 12% more reviews overall.

**Use SMS and WhatsApp.** Email open rates in India are abysmal. Send a brief WhatsApp message 2-3 days after service with your review link. Keep it personal, not templated.

**Never incentivise.** "Get 10% off your next visit for a review" violates policy. "We'd appreciate your feedback on Google" is fine.

## LocalBusiness Schema Markup with India-Specific Fields

Schema markup helps Google understand your business details unambiguously. While Google claims schema isn't a ranking factor, businesses with proper structured data see higher click-through rates from rich results.

### Essential Schema Properties

Your LocalBusiness (or more specific type like Restaurant, MedicalClinic, EducationalOrganization) schema should include:

```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "LocalBusiness",
  "name": "Your Business Name",
  "address": {
    "@type": "PostalAddress",
    "streetAddress": "Shop 15, ABC Market",
    "addressLocality": "Noida",
    "addressRegion": "Uttar Pradesh",
    "postalCode": "201301",
    "addressCountry": "IN"
  },
  "geo": {
    "@type": "GeoCoordinates",
    "latitude": "28.5355",
    "longitude": "77.3910"
  },
  "telephone": "+91-120-XXXXXXX",
  "priceRange": "₹₹",
  "openingHoursSpecification": [...],
  "aggregateRating": {...}
}
```

### India-Specific Considerations

**Use correct country code:** "IN" for addressCountry, "+91" for telephone.

**Include full state names:** "Uttar Pradesh" not "UP" — Google's parser handles full names better.

**Add GST number where applicable:** While not a standard schema property, you can include it in the description or a custom property for B2B visibility.

**Multiple locations:** Each branch needs its own schema with unique identifiers. Don't duplicate schema across location pages.

## Voice Search and "Near Me" Queries in Hindi-Speaking Markets

Voice search changes the game for local businesses. Users don't type "best biryani restaurant Lucknow" into their phones — they ask "Lucknow mein sabse accha biryani kahan milega?"

### Voice Search Statistics That Matter

Google Assistant processes over 200 million Hindi voice queries monthly in India. In tier-2 and tier-3 cities, voice search adoption outpaces metro areas — typing in English remains a barrier, speaking in Hindi doesn't. By 2026, an estimated 35% of all local searches in Hindi-speaking states come from voice.

### Optimising for Voice in Indian Languages

**Answer questions directly.** Voice search results pull from featured snippets. Structure content as Q&A. "What are the charges for AC repair in Noida?" should have a clear answer in your service page or FAQ.

**Target conversational Hindi-English mix.** Most Indian voice queries use Hinglish: "Gurgaon mein good orthodontist kaun hai?" Your content can include these natural phrases without feeling forced.

**Claim Google Business Profile in Hindi.** You can add business descriptions and posts in Hindi. This helps Google match Hindi voice queries to your listing.

**Focus on "near me" with context.** Users say "coffee shop near me" but Google interprets location from their device. Ensure your address and service areas are accurate — you'll appear for nearby voice searches automatically.

**Local landmarks help.** Many Indians give directions by landmark. If you're near "Sector 18 Metro Station" or "City Hospital", mention these on your website and GBP.
